# Login design revision — 2026-10-12

The login now uses a white page, slim aligned BizStock/Back to home header, centered illustration-left/form-right composition, compact role guidance, password visibility and full-width Sign in, plus a plain workspace footer. No contact details, registration, legal links, social/QR/OTP controls were invented or copied from the references. Below 900px the illustration is hidden and the form is prioritized. Required-password-change Account retains its existing restricted layout; the improved unrestricted account and History/movement/dashboard implementations remain intact.

The new image is an original professional 3D inventory render generated with the **built-in imagegen tool**, with shelves/cartons/scanner/tablet and abstract indicators, no people/logos/watermark or baked text. Source `exec-b130248d-927f-4f97-bc95-df961992dc85.png` (1536×1024), generated output directory `01a12a28-e4ad-7b81-aeb1-15442f6df888`, copied privately to `.tools/login-inventory-original.png`. Local public assets are `public/assets/images/login-inventory-{480,960,1280}.webp`: 13,784 / 32,476 / 45,810 bytes. PHP GD resized/re-encoded the source at quality 80; responsive srcset/sizes and intrinsic dimensions are provided. The teal panel/HTML copy and a neutral fallback message remain available if the illustration fails. No homepage asset was reused, cropped or recolored.

Headline and supporting text are HTML: “Your inventory. Under control.” and “Track stock, manage your team and keep your business moving.” Role controls announce the selected short guidance and focus username without clearing either credential. Only username/password reach authentication; existing CSRF, persistent rate limits/countdown, validation, sessions, password changes and database-derived authorization are unchanged. All URLs/assets support APP_BASE_PATH.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a NEW original professional 3D inventory illustration for the BizStock business login page. No reference images, no reuse of any previous stockroom photographs. A clean isometric composition with one organized navy metal shelving unit, neatly stacked warm beige cartons in varying sizes, a realistic barcode scanner resting on a simple counter and a slim tablet with abstract blue/teal stock indicator rectangles and bars (no readable letters or numbers). Mature polished product-render style for adult small-business owners, physically plausible matte surfaces, soft ambient shadows, restrained navy, teal, muted blue and warm neutral palette. Uncluttered isolated scene on a light misty teal background, comfortable negative space, landscape 3:2 composition, all objects fully in frame. No characters or people, no toy-like styling, no third-party logos, no watermarks, no text or typography baked into the image. Headline will be separate HTML. Output one high quality illustration.
