# Stock integrity and safe retries

Stage 8 uses one StockMovementService for business movements and new development seeds. AtomicWrite owns the transaction, locks/rereads the active authorized actor/version/forced flag, and retries entire transactions at most three times for actual deadlock/lock timeout (short jitter; exhausted retries return 503/Retry-After). Actor locking serializes same-actor operation reservations. OperationRepository enforces actor/key uniqueness across operation kinds, canonical normalized payload hashes and durable original response snapshots. A changed payload/kind returns 409. Replays recheck current authorization and never change stock; their returned balance is the original snapshot, so clients must reload live availability.

Normal movements capture current database price/time/actor. Full Admin-only reversals derive original product, opposite type, full quantity and captured price, require a reason, reject reversals of reversals, duplicates, insufficient balance and archived products. Product rows are locked before availability/version checks and append/update; current locking reads plus unique correction links resolve parallel reversals. All errors roll back movement, cached balance/version and reservation together. History queries never write records or expose request hashes/keys. No edit/delete history API and no quantity triggers.

recordInTransaction/appendInTransaction are internal caller-owned primitives: a trusted caller supplies the locked authorized actor and owns the outer transaction. They never start/commit nested work. Stage 9 zero-product creation reserves one product-create key, append optional opening IN through the same writer, audit and complete its durable result in that transaction. Seed accounts become forced-change before their transaction is visible; installed seeds/accounts are never rewritten.
